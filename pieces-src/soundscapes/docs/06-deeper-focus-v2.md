# 06 — Deeper Focus, second take (2026-07-10)

The first Deeper Focus (D dorian, 60 bpm, tidal — removed 2026-07-10) was
Focus, but darker. Joel's actual reference was a soundscapes app: minimal 
techno, not ambient. So this take started from measurement instead of mood: 
six 60-second segments of the real thing were analyzed (tempo, onset grids, 
note transcription — pipeline and findings in `research/`), and the scene is 
composed from what the analysis verified.

## What the measurements dictated

- **120.05 bpm, one sixteenth grid.** The felt pulse is 60 — quarter-note
  sub bass reading as a resting heartbeat. We use 120 exactly.
- **The pulse pattern Joel heard by ear**, confirmed in the quantized sub
  band: three bars of plain quarters, fourth bar's last beat split into
  eighths (pulse-a), and the recharge variant with a ghost sixteenth on the
  "a" of beat one (pulse-b). The night segments displace the pulse to the
  offbeat — that's ticks-b.
- **Harmony is timbre.** Root C for the whole session; palette C–D–Eb–G–Bb
  (minor pentatonic plus the ninth); the "chord" of the reference is the
  overtone series of one sustained C. The bed voices exactly that: organ
  anchor C2+G2, pad groups carrying E4 (5th partial) and Bb3 (7th) in turn.
- **Layer personalities differ by grid discipline**: mids hard-quantized
  (blips dead on the sixteenth grid, echoes at a dotted eighth), bass loose
  and slidey (32nd-note semitone approach tones standing in for 303 slides),
  hats straight eighths.
- **Change by texture, not loudness**: the conductor swaps variants and
  rests layers; nothing crescendos.

## Differences from the reference, on purpose

- All-new material — the analysis gave parameters and pattern language,
  not notes to copy (and only the two-bar pulse skeleton is even close to
  quotable).
- GM patches instead of Hawtin's sound design: the sub-heavy spectral tilt
  is approximated with volume staging (CC7: blips 52, ticks 55 vs pulse
  100), not filters. This is the same timbre risk the whole soundscapes
  project carries (docs/04) and the first thing to revisit on feedback.
- Loop lengths 16/8/10/6/14 bars (LCM 1680 bars ≈ 56 min) — Eno drift on a
  locked grid, where the reference pre-renders a full day server-side.

## Open questions for the listening pass

1. Does the pulse read as a heartbeat or as a GM bass demo?
2. Are the blips too loud/bright as square lead, or right once the volume
   staging settles?
3. Should the acid voice slide (pitch-bend support in lib) instead of
   grace-noting?
4. Is ticks-a's eighth-note hat too present for a focus room?
