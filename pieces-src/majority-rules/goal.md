# Goal

Compose **"Majority Rules"** — a new Dixieland street-band piece in
B-flat major, "the people's key." It should have the gleefully obnoxious
personality of *High Street Riot* but the seven walking players of *Cut
Loose*: cornet, clarinet, alto sax, trombone, sousaphone, snare drum, and
bass drum with mounted cymbal. No piano, banjo, or extra horns.

The band has mistaken a town meeting for a gig. Every player proposes a
motion, interrupts the others, and demands a vote; the sousaphone keeps the
minutes by hauling every digression back to B-flat. The form is a civic
rondo — motion, amendment, objection, opposition, filibuster, roll call,
unanimous shout — rather than another vamp lifecycle or a funeral/second-line
transformation.

Make it fun, melodic, idiomatic, and playable. The comedy must be in the
written music: interruptions, stop-time objections, different instrumental
characters, false endings, and the moment several independent ideas become
one shout chorus. Generate playable MIDI, render it, measure the arc, export
an engraved score, and package it for the listening room.

## Constraints

1. B-flat major is home and wins the vote. Brief trips to its close relations
   are allowed, but the tonic must feel inevitable rather than merely stated.
2. About 3.5–4.5 minutes; one continuous movement at a dancing street tempo.
3. Use exactly the *Cut Loose* roster, with the two drummers treated as two
   players.
4. Give each horn an authored speaking role, then combine the motion and the
   amendment at the climax.
5. Deterministic build, range-clean, playable, rendered, and web-packaged.
