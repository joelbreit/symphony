# Blueprint — the procession as form

4/4 throughout. One tempo change that matters (♩ = 66 → 190), one push
(→ 196), and a return to the walk for the benediction. 176 bars ≈ 5:00.

## The hymn — 16 bars, E♭

Chart (one chord per bar; `(x, y)` splits the bar):

    E♭ E♭ A♭ B♭7 | E♭ E♭ B♭7 E♭ | E♭7 A♭ A♭m (E♭/B♭,B♭7) | E♭ C7 (F7,B♭7) E♭

Melody (concert):

    G4 B♭4 E♭5—   | D5. C5 B♭4—   | C5 A♭4 B♭4 G4 | F4—. ·
    G4 B♭4 E♭5—   | D5. C5 B♭4 G4 | F4 G4 A♭4 F4  | E♭4—. ·
    B♭4 E♭5 G5—   | F5 E♭5 C5—    | C♭5—. B♭4     | A♭4 G4 F4—
    G4 B♭4 E♭5—   | E5. D5 C5—    | A4 C5 B♭4 D5  | E♭5——

Phrase 1 the call and a half cadence; phrase 2 the call again, settling
low; phrase 3 the climb to G5 and the tear (C → C♭ over A♭ → A♭m); phrase
4 the call once more through the rag chain (E♮ over C7, A♮ over F7) up to
the high E♭. The **call** — G B♭ E♭ D C B♭ — is the piece's cell.

## The strut — the same 16 bars at ♩ = 190

Same pitches; the long notes moved onto the *and* of 2 and held, pickups
and chromatic walk-ups (G–A♭–A–B♭) filling the seams, the fourth beat of
bars 4, 8 and 12 left open for the clarinet. Chart as the hymn's, with
B♭7 turnarounds in bars 8 and 16 when it repeats. The out-chorus variant
takes the climb to B♭5 instead of G5.

## The ramble — 16 bars, A♭

    A♭ A♭ E♭7 E♭7 | E♭7 E♭7 A♭ (A♭,A♭7) | D♭ D♭ D♭m A♭/E♭ | F7 B♭7 E♭7 A♭

A riff tune: the bouncing triad (· E♭ C A♭ · C E♭) on I, its answer on
V7, a chromatic lean (A♭–B♭–B♮–C) between them, stop-time in bars 7–8,
the D♭ → D♭m tear in bar 11 (F → F♭), and a chromatic walk-up into the
rag chain home. Trombone and clarinet each get a chorus on it.

## The shout — 16 bars, A♭, block harmony

The hymn's call transposed to A♭ (C E♭ A♭· G | F E♭ ·) shouted by cornet
with clarinet above and alto below on chord tones, the drummers answering
in beats 3–4 of bars 2, 4, 6, 8; from bar 9 the call goes up to D♭6, the
tear (F♭) in bar 11, and the rag chain home.

## Texture rules

- Cornet: nothing before bar 45. Not a stab, not a pad.
- Dirge lead lines carry CC1 ≈ 100; everything after the whistle CC1 = 0.
- One front-line voice in eighths at a time in the hot sections.
- Sousaphone walks into every fourth bar; four-to-the-bar only from
  bar 158 (the second half of Home).
- Drums: cadence (bars 1–32), street beat (34 on), fills every fourth bar
  from the ramble on, breaks at 80–81, 170.
- Tempo 190 flat through the ramble; 196 from the shout.
