# Musical Materials

## Key plan

- **Home: E♭ major** — the heroic key (Eroica, Emperor Concerto). Warm in brass, rich in strings.
- **The work / the night: C minor** (relative minor — same world, lights off).
- **The private voice: A♭ major** (subdominant — the warm, inward direction).
- **The awe chord: C♭ major (♭VI of E♭)**, reached as a deceptive resolution from B♭7.
  Enharmonically B major — the most luminous-alien triad available to E♭. This is the
  sunrise. Used exactly once.
- Connective trick: section III ends on G7 (V of C minor) resolving deceptively to
  **A♭** — the work's energy melts directly into the private voice's key.

## Motif 1 — The Summons (horn call)

`B♭3 – E♭4 – F4 – G4` (rising P4, then two steps; long–short–short–long).
Opens the piece, becomes the head of the anthem, gets inverted (falling 4th = the
call failing) in the night, returns once at the end, far off. Everything grows from this.

## Motif 3 — The Doubt theme (the private voice)

**Composed against Strain A's changes** so it overlays in counterpoint at the
apotheosis. Long values against the anthem's dotted quarters; contrary or oblique
motion; converges at cadences. In E♭ (overlay form):

| bar | counter-melody (vs. anthem) |
|---|---|
| 1 | G5(h.) F5(q) — starts high, falls as anthem rises |
| 2 | E♭5(h) D5(q) C5(q) |
| 3 | C5(q.) D5(e) E♭5(h) — rises back as anthem crests/falls |
| 4 | D5(h.) B♭4(q) — converge on V |
| 5 | G4(h.) A♭4(q) — drops below; anthem head sails above |
| 6 | A♭4(h) G4(q) F4(q) |
| 7 | F4(q.) G4(e) A♭4(q) B♭4(q) — contrary vs. anthem's descent |
| 8 | G4(w) — lands on the 3rd while anthem lands on the root |

**Solo form** (section IV): same line transposed down a 5th into A♭ major, sung by
oboe over `I | vi | IV | V` in A♭, ornamented, extended 8 more bars (flute takes it
an octave up, small climax, sigh-off). The listener hears it as "the oboe's song";
at the apotheosis they discover it was the anthem's partner all along.

## Motif 4 — The Bells (change-ring)

`G5 – E♭5 – F5 – B♭4` (3̂–1̂–2̂–5̂ descending chime). Tubular bells + glockenspiel from
the sunrise onward, pealing between anthem phrases.

## Section III material — The Work (C minor, motoric)

- 8th-note ostinato cell in inner strings: `G4 E♭4 G4 C5 | G4 F4 G4 B♮4` with offbeat accents.
- Walking quarter bass; snare comping.
- The summons motif in **stretto imitation** (clarinet → oboe → flute → trumpet, one
  beat apart) — many hands, same gesture.
- Builds by accumulation every 4 bars; brightens to E♭ (relative major = the work
  bearing fruit); **hemiola** (3-beat pattern across two 4/4 bars) into the cadence;
  G7 → deceptive A♭ (= section IV).

## Section V material — The Long Night (C minor)

- **Lament tetrachord ground bass**: C – B♭ – A♮ – A♭ – G, the traditional grief
  emblem, looping under fragmented, minor-warped doubt-theme.
- Summons inverted (falling 4th) in muted trumpet.
- Diminished sequence climbing by minor 3rds, chromatic string ascent, snare roll.
- German 6th (C♭–E♭–G♭–A♮) → I6/4 → B♭7 pedal, fff → **G.P. — one full bar of
  silence** → C♭ major sunrise (deceptive V7→♭VI), pp swelling to ff, first bell.
